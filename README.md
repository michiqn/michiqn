# Hey, I'm Michi 👋

I like taking an idea from the first sketch to a prototype that actually works, and learning whatever I need on the way.
I'm curious about new technologies and see AI as an enabler for learning new skills. As a beginner in the hardware
and maker world, that helps a lot. Claude mainly helps me develop the software that brings my hardware projects to life.

Here you'll find my private projects, mostly robotics and 3D printing, and some of my university work.

🎓 Master's student in Management & Technology at **TUM** (Munich), currently writing my thesis.

### 🤖 Robotics
* **[handlab](https://github.com/michiqn/handlab)**: Control center and live MuJoCo digital twin for tendon driven
  robotic hands. Dynamixel servo control, WiLoR hand tracking teleoperation, ArUco vision, and Claude driving the hand via MCP.
* **[handlab hardware](https://github.com/michiqn/handlab-hardware)**: The 3 and 4 finger tendon driven hands:
  CAD, photos, build notes.
* **[KraQn](https://github.com/michiqn/KraQn)**: My first robotics project. A two tendon logarithmic spiral arm
  inspired by SpiRobs, with my own body, base and electronics.

### 🛠️ Embedded & iOS
* **[TrainBrain](https://github.com/michiqn/trainbrain)**: A small e ink display with a rotary knob, controlled
  from my iPhone over Bluetooth LE. Used as a workout counter and vocabulary quiz.

### 🎓 University projects
* **[neuvia](https://github.com/michiqn/neuvia)**: A notebook that turns your learning journey into an interactive

* **[Math Problem Helper](https://github.com/noahlatzel/human-ai-interaction)**: Learning platform that helps
